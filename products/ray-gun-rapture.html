Ray-Gun Rapture

Setting Overview

Neon Lights & Impossible Dreams

Neon lights flicker through Europa Colony's perpetual twilight. Art deco structures defy the laws of physics while gramophone music drifts from bustling cafés, mingling with the hiss of rocket exhaust and the crackle of ray-gun discharge. This is Ray-Gun Rapture — where the boundless optimism of the atomic age collides with the cold realities of colonial politics and the mysteries lurking at the edges of known space.

Colonial society is divided into seven distinct zones, each reflecting a different approach to civilization — from the gleaming spires of the administrative quarter to the neon-soaked entertainment districts and the industrial depths where workers toil beneath the ice. Every zone has its own culture, its own power brokers, and its own secrets.

Beyond the colony's domed safety lies the vast Jovian system, where horrors beyond comprehension lurk in the gas clouds and ancient signals pulse from places that should be empty. Europa Colony is a beacon of humanity's ambition — and potentially its last outpost before the dark.

Vacuum tubes, positronic brains, and rocket ships powered by impossible science. Technology that feels both magnificently advanced and charmingly analog.

Seven zones, seven power structures, seven agendas. Navigating the social landscape of Europa Colony demands as much skill as any firefight.

Space Exploration

The Jovian system stretches outward, vast and largely unknown. Every expedition beyond the domes risks contact with things the old pulp magazines only dreamed of.
