The Concept

Pro Wrestlers. Carnival Mysteries. Supernatural Shenanigans.

All Star Wrestlers Investigate throws the biggest personalities from the 80s and 90s squared circle into a world ripped straight from Saturday morning cartoons. When spooky things start happening at carnivals and fairgrounds, there's only one team qualified to body-slam the truth out of whatever's lurking behind the curtain.

The Tone

Campy, theatrical, and gleefully over-the-top. Every mystery ends with an unmasking, every villain gets a monologue, and every wrestler has a catchphrase for the occasion. Family-friendly chaos with Saturday morning cartoon sensibilities.

The Setting

Carnivals, fairgrounds, and travelling sideshows. Strange things happen here — haunted strongmen, phantom fortune tellers, monsters that turn out to be disgruntled employees with a grudge and a fog machine.

The System

The Ribhian Games 2d10 system powers All Star Wrestlers Investigate — fast, dramatic, and built for big moments. Roll 2d10 against your stats, spend from your Fortune Pool to push harder, and rolling doubles refills it no matter what. Every session has a Surge moment waiting to happen.

The Characters

Each wrestler is built around a Gimmick — their in-ring persona and out-of-ring identity — paired with Talents that define their toolkit. From technical masterminds to airborne highflyers to supernatural outsiders, the roster covers every crowd-pleasing archetype.

Who You Are

Alignment — Face, Heel, or Tweener

Before you choose your Gimmick, you choose your alignment — your relationship with the crowd. It shapes your Work Pool, your Heat track, and how the world reacts to everything you do inside and outside the ring.

🎭 Choose Your Relationship With The Crowd
⭐
The Face
"The crowd is my power. Every cheer fills me up. Every boo makes me stronger."
The hero. The crowd favourite. You fight with integrity, defend the weak, and give the people what they came to see. Your strength comes from the audience — when they believe in you, you are genuinely unstoppable.
Heat +5 or higher Work Pool +2 Pop bonus active
Alignment Benefit Strong Face (+5 or higher Heat): +2 Work Pool. Crowd cheers restore Fortune Pool on doubles.
💀
The Heel
"Your boos? That's music to me. Keep hating — it means I'm doing my job."
The villain. The crowd hates you and that's exactly how you like it. You cheat, you manipulate, you take shortcuts — and you thrive on every boo and insult hurled from the stands. Heel Heat is still Heat.
Heat -5 or lower Work Pool +2 Intimidation bonus
Alignment Benefit Strong Heel (-5 or lower Heat): +2 Work Pool. Cheap heat and crowd antagonism fuel your abilities.
🎭
The Tweener
"I don't care if they cheer or boo. I care if they're watching."
The wildcard. Neither hero nor villain, you operate in the grey — respected, unpredictable, and impossible to categorise. The crowd never quite knows how to feel about you, which is exactly where you want them.
Heat near 0 Work Pool +0 Flexible allegiance
Alignment Benefit Tweener (Heat 0): No Work Pool bonus, but Heat can shift rapidly — a single moment can turn you Face or Heel.

The Mystery Component

Classic Carnie Villains

Every ASWI mystery follows the golden rule: it was never actually supernatural. Behind every monster is a disgruntled employee with a grudge and access to carnival equipment. The wrestlers just have to figure out which one.

💪
The Haunted Strongman
"He's been lifting... FROM BEYOND THE GRAVE!"
A massive figure of impossible strength stalking the carnival at night, bending steel bars and lifting equipment no human should move. Witnesses report glowing eyes and an unearthly groan echoing through the midway.
🔍 True Identity The fired carnival accountant — using hydraulic assists hidden under a costume and phosphorescent face paint. The impossible lifts are rigged with counterweights.
🍬
The Cotton Candy Phantom
"Sticky pink webs everywhere. Nobody is safe."
A spectral figure that leaves trails of sticky pink webbing across the carnival, tangling rides and trapping unfortunate victims. The webbing is bizarrely resistant and smells of artificial strawberry.
🔍 True Identity The rival carnival owner — operating a heavily modified industrial cotton candy machine on a concealed cart. The supernatural webs are just very, very efficient candy floss.
🔮
The Fortune Teller's Floating Head
"Madame Mysteria sees ALL!"
A disembodied glowing head appears in the fortune teller's tent, speaking prophecies and terrifying customers. The head floats, rotates, and seems to pass through solid objects with impossible ease.
🔍 True Identity A network of mirrors and projection equipment controlled by the tent owner's nephew. The prophecies are educated guesses based on carnival gossip and local newspaper research.
🎡
The Tunnel of Love Monster
"The rides are being sabotaged. Love hurts."
A lurking figure systematically sabotaging the carnival's most beloved attraction — boats stall, lights flicker, terrifying sounds echo through the darkness. The sabotage is elaborate and deeply personal.
🔍 True Identity The heartbroken maintenance worker whose partner left them for someone they met on the very ride they now haunt. Precision sabotage. Very relatable, honestly.
💨
The Human Cannonball Ghost
"Flying through the air... mysteriously."
A glowing figure soars overhead between the carnival tents at night performing impossible aerial maneuvers. No cannon fires. No net catches it. It simply flies — and leaves no footprints on landing.
🔍 True Identity The disgraced stunt coordinator — using hidden zip lines installed during the off-season, a luminescent suit, and precisely timed launches from a concealed compressed air device.

How It Plays

The Classic Mystery Format

Every ASWI adventure follows the seven-beat structure that made Saturday morning cartoons legendary. From the opening hook to the final unmasking, the formula never gets old.

1

Setup

The wrestlers arrive at the carnival for a charity wrestling match. Everything seems perfectly normal — suspiciously normal.

2

The Hook

A spooky supernatural event occurs. The carnival owner, frantic and desperate, begs the only people around who can help.

3

Investigation

Split up to search for clues. Classic cartoon corridor chases ensue. Suspects are identified. Red herrings abound.

4

Confrontation

A wrestling match against the monster and their henchmen. The ring is the great equaliser.

5

The Trap

An elaborate scheme combining wrestling moves with carnival equipment. Timing, teamwork, and spectacle required.

6

Unmasking

"Old Man Jenkins! The Dunking Booth Operator!" The monster is revealed — and it's always someone with a motive.

7

Confession

"And I would've gotten away with it too, if it weren't for you meddling wrestlers!" Curtain falls. Catchphrases delivered.
