Thirty Years in the Making

Heroes of Ribhus: Legacy

Heroes of Ribhus: Legacy is the original Ribhus organized play campaign — a high fantasy setting that has been running at conventions and home tables for over thirty years. It is set in the same world as Chronicles of Ribhus but 235 years in the past, during the formative age when the great powers of Ribhus were still being forged and the world's fate was genuinely unwritten.

This is where the Ribhus story begins. The lore you encounter in Chronicles of Ribhus — the fallen kingdoms, the ancient wars, the legendary heroes — all of it has its roots in the events that players are shaping right now in Legacy. Play here and you are writing the history that the future remembers.

Heroes of Ribhus: Legacy runs on D&D 5th Edition (2014) Variant under the Open Game License 1.0, with extensive campaign-specific expansions including unique classes, modified races, regional skill sets, new feats, and the Reputation mechanic — a system tracking your Fame and Infamy across the world of Ribhus and shaping how every faction, city, and NPC responds to your presence.

⚖ Licensed under Open Game License 1.0  ·  Requires D&D 5E (2014) Player's Handbook

The campaign is designed for organized play — structured events at conventions and game days where multiple tables run simultaneously, characters are persistent across events, and the actions of players collectively shape the ongoing storyline of the world. Time Units track your character's advancement between events. Reputation follows you everywhere you go.

The Campaign

How It Works

Organized & Home Play

Heroes of Ribhus Legacy is built for both convention organized play and home campaign use. Characters are persistent across events — your decisions, your reputation, and your history follow you from table to table, venue to venue, and session to session.

Time Units

Earned Time Units (ETU) are awarded for each event, averaging 6 per event. Every 3 ETU grants 1 Available Time Unit (ATU) for item creation, feat acquisition, and organizational membership between adventures.

The Setting

The world of Ribhus — 235 years before the events of Chronicles of Ribhus. The great cities are rising, the ancient powers are active, and the history that Chronicles references as legend is happening now at your table.

D&D 5E Foundation

Built on the 2014 D&D 5th Edition ruleset under OGL 1.0, with campaign-specific expansions. Unique classes, modified races, regional skill sets, and new feats sit alongside the familiar 5E framework.

The Signature Mechanic

Reputation — Fame & Infamy

Reputation is the Heroes of Ribhus campaign's central social mechanic. Every character maintains a Fame score and an Infamy score — two separate measures of how the world knows you. For every 10 points of Fame and/or Infamy you accumulate, your Reputation increases by +1.

Reputation is a two-edged sword. The same score that gets you a discount at the merchant's table will also make a bounty hunter's day when they recognize your face. The higher your Reputation, the wider it spreads — and the harder it becomes to go anywhere unnoticed.

Race, class, and actions all contribute to your starting scores. Extraordinary acts of heroism generate Fame. Acts of darkness and violence generate Infamy. Everyday actions generate nothing — only deeds worth remembering move the needle.

0–3
Unknown
The world has not yet heard your name. You move freely, unnoticed — which is both a shelter and a limitation. No doors open on reputation alone.
4–6
Local Hero
Word of your deeds has spread within a region. Innkeepers offer a free round. Merchants shave their prices. The wrong people are starting to hear your name too.
7–9
Famous / Infamous
Your name crosses city lines. Noble houses grant audiences. Guild factors seek introductions. Rivals across the region have marked you as someone worth watching — or removing.
10–13
Well-Known
Strangers recognise you on the road. Stories about you are already half-legend and half-invention. Those stories have a way of making both your life easier and significantly more dangerous.
14–17
Renowned
The great powers of Ribhus know your face. You are welcomed at the highest tables — and hunted by the darkest corners. Privacy is a luxury you can no longer afford.
18–20
Legendary
Your deeds are already being written into the history that Chronicles of Ribhus will one day call legend. The world bends around your presence. So do its most powerful enemies.
⭐ Fame
Earned through completing heroic quests, public performances, acts of charity, service to temples, and service to the nobility. Fame opens doors in high society, earns better prices from merchants, and generates favorable treatment from authorities.
💀 Infamy
Earned through criminal activities, violent solutions, dark pacts, intimidating displays, and association with feared groups. Infamy makes commoners avoid you, authorities suspicious — but some criminals offer assistance, and Intimidation checks carry natural weight.
