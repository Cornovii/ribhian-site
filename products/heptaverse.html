What is the Heptaverse?

Seven Worlds. One Tapestry.

Step into an adventure that spans dimensions, where actions in one reality ripple across the cosmic tapestry. The Heptaverse is a shared cosmological framework connecting seven distinct game settings — each with its own genre, tone, and unique mechanics — all powered by the same 2d10 system. Characters can travel between worlds. Choices in one setting echo across all others. Where will your journey begin?

The Seven Worlds

Choose Your Reality

High Fantasy

Chronicles of Ribhus

Ancient forests whisper secrets to those who know how to listen. The original realm of the Heptaverse, where high fantasy meets cosmic design. Nations compete, ancient magical traditions clash, and good and evil are rarely clearly defined.

Runic Concordance magic · Political intrigue · Eight moral alignments · Diverse races

Celtic Iron Age

Blood & Stone

Stone circles pulse with ancestral power as tribal confederations face the advance of southern kingdoms. Ritual scarification creates telepathic clan networks. Sacred sites fall to "civilizing" forces.

Confederation politics · Stone circle magic · Fey alliances · Cultural preservation

Victorian Gothic Horror

Tendrils in the Fog

Gas lamps struggle against perpetual fog in New Cambridge. Three founding families conduct mysterious industrial projects while respectable Victorian society conceals cosmic horror beneath its proper veneer.

Industrial mysticism · Family bloodline power · Resonance currency · Reality integration

Mayan Steampunk

The Core

Brass gears turn with impossible precision on Nexus Prime, near the galactic core. Engineers maintain the Great Server — reality itself. What appears to be technology is actually cosmic ritual disguised as engineering.

Reality maintenance · Dual cultures · Cosmic infrastructure · Multiverse coordination

Weird West

Madness & Dust

Dust swirls across the American frontier as reality bends at the edges of perception. Strange phenomena plague isolated towns while the Deck of Fortune determines fate itself. Three celestial bodies hang in impossible configurations.

Fortune manifestation · Frontier survival · Cosmic horror · Reality distortions

Retro Sci-Fi

Ray Gun Rapture

Neon lights flicker through Europa Colony's perpetual twilight. Art deco structures defy physics while gramophone music drifts from bustling cafés. Seven distinct zones each reflect different approaches to civilisation.

Pulp adventure · Retro-futuristic technology · Colonial politics · Space exploration

Dystopian Noir

Spiral Descent

Corporate arcologies tower within Ceres' habitation dome. Time itself has become currency while rare genetic mutations produce individuals with inexplicable abilities. Seven corporations control every aspect of life.

Time currency economy · Signal/Noise spectrum · Corporate warfare · Cyberpunk technology

What Binds the Worlds

The Cosmic Connections

⏳

The City of Cornovii

A wheel-shaped metropolis that travels through space, time, and all seven worlds. It appears in each realm once every century — for exactly seven days. Those who miss it wait a lifetime for the next arrival.

🌙

The Dreamscape

The collective unconscious spans all seven worlds simultaneously. Dreamers in one reality may encounter the sleeping minds of beings from another, sharing visions and warnings across the cosmic divide.

🪞

The Mirrorverse

A perfect reflection of all seven realities, showing alternate possibilities and divergent timelines. What is decided in one world echoes here — and sometimes those echoes find their way back.
